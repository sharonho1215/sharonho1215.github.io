I am an FY1 doctor at Addenbrooke’s Hospital with an MEng in Bioengineering and Information Engineering from the University of Cambridge. My research uses signal processing, statistics and machine learning to turn physiological and clinical data into tools clinicians can use. I am in a unique position where I can read both the medical language and the engineering language and I hope to combine Medicine and Engineering to transform healthcare.
